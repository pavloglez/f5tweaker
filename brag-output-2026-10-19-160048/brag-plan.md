Poco F5 Tweaker is an open-source Android hardware and kernel tuning suite for the Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2 (`marble`), unlocking locked 120Hz displays, Vulkan SkiaVK acceleration, and MIUI thermal limit overrides via Shizuku without root.

## The angle
A sleek, premium project banner video and high-resolution still banner celebrating the uncaged power of the Poco F5. Designed for repository headers, social cards, and launch posts.
